## Differs from the bible
- The evaluation bar may now be 100 plays, not 250 (DevForum "Making Publishing Easier"). Not confirmed live: read the Audience Reach dashboard.
- "Highly engaged" depends on account tenure, playtime in the game, and a minimum purchase anywhere on Roblox in the same 60 days.
- One-time refundable publishing fee: 1,000 Robux per game (snippet only). Refunded 90 days after the game becomes eligible.
- Optional expedited review that skips the bar: 50,000 Robux, refundable (docs mirror).
- Whether ad-driven plays count toward the bar: could not confirm.
- Benchmarks (day-1 10.3% median etc.) are GameAnalytics figures, not Roblox's. Top-1% session length there is 21.1 min; the bible's 16.6 is its top 5% figure and was not checked.
- Ranking windows are Day 1, Days 2-7 and Days 8-28, as the bible says. Ad-acquired users do not count (confirmed in the docs mirror).
- Look-alike penalty is documented: metadata and place files that closely resemble existing games are not prioritised.

## Content rating
- Minimal or Mild reaches Roblox Kids (5-8) and Select (9-15). Moderate does not reach Kids.
- 16+ only: free-form user creation, social hangout, media sharing. 13+: cross-experience content.
- The questionnaire asks about violence, fear, gambling, purchases, social hangout, free-form creation, media sharing, media feeds.
- A non-violent "cheer up" theme has nothing obvious that triggers a violence or fear answer. Unverified until the questionnaire is filled in.
- Mailbox codes are typed text. They go only to the server and are never shown to other players; confirm this does not count as free-form creation.

## Money rules that touch this game
- Paid random items must show all outcomes and odds and respect `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted`. This game sells none.
- The free Mystery Egg is not paid, but its odds are shown anyway (bible B7).
- Rewarding likes or favourites is treated as prohibited. The game does not do it.
- Moments (clip sharing) was 13+ at launch. Captures age limit: could not confirm. The code offers a capture only when the policy check passes.

## Neighbours (third-party coverage only)
- Battle Pets: battle wild creatures across islands, capture them, strengthen a team.
- Catch and Tame: lasso animals across biomes, pen them for passive income, breed toward rarer ones. About 216M visits.
- Climb HILL for Animals: climb a hillside to find rarer animals that earn cash. About 4.8M visits (April 2026).
How this game plays differently:
1. A catch is a three-beat timing tap: skill, not a lasso, a battle or a walk-up grab.
2. The climb is a shared, non-combat "cheer up the Guardian" with other players, and rare pets are played with together before anyone can catch them.
3. Pets ride in a visible stack on your head, and Share-or-Keep marks you as Dawn or Dusk.
