# Creator outreach (DRAFT for the owner)

Status: draft. The owner sends every message and decides any spend. Nothing here has been sent.
Rules kept in every draft: no payment offer, no player numbers, no promise of views or growth, no
pressure, no reward for likes or ratings, no other game named. Each message is under 120 words.

What we can truthfully offer (bible B7, C4)
- A creator code. A code gives a look or at most 10 minutes of income. It goes in the camp Mailbox.
- A first look at each Saturday update.
- Three 15-second vertical clips a week that the creator may use.

Fill every [bracket] before sending. Delete the age line once all ages can play.

## 1. First contact

```
Hi [Creator name],

I'm [Owner name]. I make a small Roblox game called [Game title].

You catch pets by tapping three beats in time. They ride in a wobbling stack on your head.
Then you climb a tower with other players and cheer up a grumpy Guardian on each floor.

I thought of you because of [specific video or thing you liked].

If you'd like a look, I can set up a code for your viewers, [CREATORCODE], and show you each
Saturday update early. Play it or skip it, and say what you really think.

[Right now the game is open to players aged 16 and over.]

Game: [Roblox game link]

Thanks for reading,
[Owner name]
```

## Notes for the owner

- Send from the owner's own account or email. Links belong in these messages only, never in the game.
- Check each creator's own rules for business messages before writing.
- A creator may need to tell viewers about any free code or early access. That is their call; do not
  ask them to hide it.
- Creator codes must be made in the game's code list before a message goes out (Codes service).
- Do not send message 1 until the game is published and the link works.
