# Clip shot lists for the B15 moments (DRAFT for the owner)

Status: draft. The owner approves and posts every clip. Each clip is 15 seconds, vertical (9:16).
Real gameplay only: the real build, real players or the team's own test accounts, real HUD. Nothing
staged that a player could not do. No added promises, no "like for a reward", no links burned into the
video beyond the game title. Hide other players' names unless they agreed.
The game is landscape (B12). Capture in landscape and crop to the centre, or capture on a phone held
upright if the build allows it. [Owner to choose after a test capture.]
Sound comes from the game (B11). Do not lay other music over it.
Words in "quotes" below are caption ideas. Use them as on-screen game text only if the build shows them.

## 1a. "SO CLOSE": a Legendary bolts on beat three

Setup: a Legendary on any floor. It wants to play first, so start recording near the end of the play.
- 0-3 s: pillar of light, server banner. Player runs in with a pet stack on their head.
- 3-7 s: last seconds of the wild showdown. The team plays, the Play meter empties.
- 7-10 s: Trust Tap. Ring one: Perfect. Ring two: Perfect.
- 10-11 s: ring three: Miss.
- 11-14 s: the pet bolts in slow motion.
- 14-15 s: player stands alone under the fading pillar. Nothing is lost.
On screen: pillar of light, the three rings, Perfect/Miss marks, the bolt.
Sound: three rising notes, the third cut short; slide-whistle on the bolt.

## 1b. "PERFECT x3" under the pillar of light (the mirror)

- 0-3 s: same opening: pillar, banner, run in.
- 3-7 s: end of the play. Meter empties.
- 7-11 s: three rings, three Perfects.
- 11-13 s: catch sparkle. The Legendary hops onto the stack.
- 13-15 s: the stack wobbles with the new pet on top.
On screen: pillar, rings, three Perfect marks, gold rarity frame, the stack.
Sound: three rising notes resolving on the catch jingle.

## 2. The Dawn-or-Dusk evolution reveal

Setup: a starter at Lv 41, with the coins for one more feed. Record once for Dawn and once for Dusk.
- 0-3 s: camp. Player feeds the starter at the Snack Table. Level ticks to 42.
- 3-6 s: the two form choices appear, gold and violet. Hold on them.
- 6-7 s: the player picks one.
- 7-10 s: the full evolution moment: flash, fanfare, camera orbit (2.5 s).
- 10-13 s: the new form stands in its palette: sun crest and rays, or crescent crest and star sparkles.
- 13-15 s: the player's aura and Team Power number with the new form in front.
On screen: the level number, both choices, the whole evolution without cuts.
Sound: level-up jingle, then the evolution fanfare. Check the clip once with reduced flash on.

## 3. The pet stack: ten pets on a tiny avatar

Setup: stack size 10 (the maximum), on any floor with good colour.
- 0-2 s: close on the avatar with seven or more pets stacked.
- 2-8 s: three quick catches. Each pet hops on top. The stack grows to ten.
- 8-12 s: the player runs and jumps toward the lift. The stack wobbles.
- 12-15 s: wide shot: tiny avatar, tall stack, the tower behind. Wick points home.
On screen: the whole stack in frame top to bottom, at least three rarity colours.
Sound: catch jingles, coin pops, the floor's calm loop underneath.

## 4. [A1] A whole server plays with one Legendary, then seven catch tries at once

Setup: seven players on one floor when a Legendary appears. All step into the ring.
- 0-2 s: pillar of light and banner. Seven players ride up or run in.
- 2-8 s: all seven teams play with the pet. The Play meter drains fast.
- 8-9 s: meter empty.
- 9-13 s: seven sets of rings at once. Hits and misses all around.
- 13-15 s: sparkles for the catches, slow-motion bolt for the misses, players' stacks wobbling.
On screen: all seven players, the Play meter, the pillar. Pick a camera spot that sees the whole ring.
Sound: showdown music, rising ring notes overlapping, catch jingles and one slide-whistle.

## 5. Seven players' pets around one building-sized Guardian

Setup: a floor the recording account has not cleared, seven players in the ring. Showdowns last 40 s,
so record the last part.
- 0-3 s: low angle. The Guardian fills the frame, grumpy or sleepy. The floor is dim.
- 3-9 s: 21 pets dance, bounce and sparkle around it. Players tap rings to cheer. The meter runs down.
- 9-11 s: meter empty. The Guardian smiles.
- 11-14 s: the lantern relights and the floor blooms into full colour.
- 14-15 s: the Guardian waves. The Share and Keep buttons appear.
On screen: the size gap between pets and Guardian, the lantern, the colour bloom in one unbroken shot.
Sound: faster showdown music, cheer hits, then the lantern relight swell.
