# Saturday patch notes (DRAFT template and example for the owner)

Status: draft. The owner approves and posts every patch note. Nothing here is published.
Rules: short sentences a 7-year-old can follow. Only list what is in the build. No "last chance", no
countdowns, no "hurry". Events come back (B14). Never reward likes, favourites or ratings.
Each update also ships a patch-note image and three 15 s clips (B14).

## Template

```
[Game title]: Week [N] update. [Short name of the update]

NEW FLOORS
- Floor [number], [floor name]: [one line on how it looks].
- Floor [number], [floor name]: [one line on how it looks].

NEW PETS
- [Number] new pets to befriend.
- Look out for [pet name], [one line], and [pet name], [one line].

NEW GUARDIANS
- Floor [number]: [Guardian name], [grumpy, sleepy or lonely, and why].
- Weekly Guardian in the hub: [Guardian name]. Bring friends. A full ring wins comfortably.

ALSO NEW
- [Feature]: [one line].

THIS WEEK'S CODE
- [CODE]: [a look, or up to 10 minutes of income]. Type it into the Mailbox at your camp.

FIXES
- [Fix in plain words.]
- [Fix in plain words.]

Thank you for playing. See you on the tower.
```

Checks before posting
- Every number matches the build (count the pets in the Pets config).
- The code exists, works, and gives a look or at most 10 minutes of income (B7).
- No pet or Guardian name is posted before its clash check (hard rule 6).
- Prices are mentioned only if the owner has approved them.

## Open points this example shows

- Pet count: B7 says "8 new pets" each Saturday and B14 says "8 or more", but two floors at 5 pets each
  is 10, or 12 with one Secret per floor. The owner chooses the number.
- Floors 6 and 7 have no colour mood, pets, Guardians, natures or economy values in the bible yet.
- The bible names two Weekly Guardians (Honk the Clockwork Goose, Dozy the Pillow Dragon) but does not
  say which week each appears.

  shown to younger players before it ships.
