## Double-check these four in Creator Hub

1. **Digital purchases (8, 9).** Decision 24 keeps the shop off until prices are approved. If the soft
   launch build has no shop, the truthful answer may be "no" at first and must be changed when the shop
   opens. Confirm that a free, unskippable random egg is not a "paid random item" (research says it is not).
2. **Free-form user creation (11).** Research asks us to confirm that typed Mailbox codes, sent only to
   the server, do not count. If they do count, the category is 16+ only.
3. **Media sharing (15).** At clip moments the game offers a capture through the Captures API, only where
   the platform's policy check allows it for that player (B15, docs/API_CHECK.md). The capture goes to the
   player's own Roblox gallery, not to other players. Confirm how the questionnaire treats this. The
   Captures age limit is unconfirmed (research).
4. **Social hangout (10).** Confirm that a hub plaza with emotes, free private servers and platform chat
   left at its default does not fit Roblox's definition. If the owner turns platform chat off for the
   game, say so here.

Also re-read the fear and violence wording once the real Guardians and effects are in the build.
Research calls the "cheer up" theme safe but unverified until the questionnaire is filled in.

## Changes that would push the rating up (avoid)

- Pets or Guardians hitting, hurting, fainting or losing health. Any health bar on a pet (C1).
- Scary Guardians, jump scares, a floor that stays dark, realistic art (B2).
- Any paid egg, spin, crate or luck boost, or a paid skip for the Mystery Egg timer (B2).
- Player-written text shown to others: pet nicknames, camp signs, a message board, shared code entry.
- Drawing, building or decorating that other players can see beyond fixed, pre-made items.
- Turning the hub into a place to hang out with no goal (16+ only).
- Showing players' captures, clips or any video feed inside the game (16+ only).
- Teleports to, or adverts for, other experiences (13+).
- A talking AI guide, or any AI-generated chat.
- Trading, if it is ever added: review the questionnaire again before it ships (B7: none at launch).
