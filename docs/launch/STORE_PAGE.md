## Description (under 1,000 characters; the first two lines explain the game)

```
Catch pets by tapping three beats in time, then climb the Lantern Tower floor by floor.
Your pets earn coins at camp, your team grows, and together you cheer up each floor's grumpy Guardian.

- Pick 1 of 3 starter pets and watch it evolve.
- Befriend wild pets with the Trust Tap. Timing decides, not luck.
- Carry your catches home in a wobbling pet stack on your head.
- Collect [25] wild pets across [5] floors and fill your index.
- Play with friends: everyone in a showdown adds their team and gets the full reward.
- Rare pets want to play first. The whole server can join in.
- Share or Keep? Your choice lights your tower gold or violet.
- A welcome-back chest and one free Mystery Egg a day.

Nobody can take your pets. Nothing gets hurt: pets dance, bounce and sparkle.
Works with chat off.

[The shop sells looks and time-savers only. Nothing for sale is random.]
[New floors and pets on Saturdays.]
```

## Title candidates (bible C4: explains the game in 6 words or fewer; never leads with rewards)

Research found none of these in use, by web search only. Search Roblox itself before choosing.

1. **Catch Pets, Climb The Tower.** Pro: says both verbs of the game in five words; it is the working
   title. Con: "pets" and "tower" are common words, so search results will be busy.
2. **Stack Pets, Climb the Tower.** Pro: least crowded in research, and the stack is our most striking
   sight. Con: "stack" does not tell a new player that they catch pets.
3. **Lantern Tower Pets.** Pro: least crowded in research, and it is our own world name. Con: it names
   the place, not what you do.
4. **Pet Tower: Catch and Climb.** Pro: short and clear. Con: "Catch and Climb" echoes Catch and Tame,
   and "Pet Tower" sits near Pet Tower Defense and Planet Pet Tower. Close look-alikes rank lower.
5. **Tap, Catch, Climb: Pet Tower.** Pro: the only one that names the timing tap. Con: same "Pet Tower"
   crowding, and three verbs are harder to remember.

Suggested order to test: 1, then 2. The owner decides.
